# Era — Market Pains and Feature Mapping

Research brief. Source: web research on Erasmus+ project operations, June 2026. Maps common pains to Era panels (finance AKA-34, management AKA-35, activity log AKA-22).

## Core pains

**Financial audit risk.** Coordinators carry full legal liability for recoveries at final payment. National Agencies run desk checks and on-site audits. Every invoice, bank statement, and receipt must stay linked to the right activity and participant, ready on demand. Lost travel documents mean no reimbursement. Partners owe proof within ~3 months. Most reported errors come from mismatches between narrative and financial data, incomplete docs, and misread eligibility rules.

**Tool fragmentation.** Official tools (Beneficiary Module, Mobility Tool+) handle submission only. Real tracking lives in scattered Excel sheets and email. No single source of truth. SALTO ships an Excel budget template as the default.

**People and task management.** Each project needs documented task allocation across partners. Informal email agreements do not satisfy compliance. Roles, decisions, and conflict handling need a record.

**Lump-sum reporting (dominant in KA2).** Payment depends on work packages delivered as proposed. Partial delivery cuts the grant proportionally. Every result needs evidence: minutes, materials, photos, publications. Weak work-package tracking is a top cause of score loss.

**GDPR.** Personal documents get collected from day one. Photo and video use needs provable consent. Organizations must prove consent exists for every data subject.

**Deadlines.** Interim report at midpoint for projects over 24 months. Final report within 60 days of project end. Missed obligations risk termination.

## Pain to feature mapping

| Pain | Era feature | Status | Paid hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audit liability, scattered receipts | Finance panel: project-scoped entries, derived totals, per-entry document vault, Excel export | AKA-34 partial | Audit-ready export (premium) |
| Eligibility errors, narrative vs financial mismatch | Budget categories tied to EU cost types, validation flags | New | Pro |
| Tool fragmentation | Org-to-project tenancy, one source of truth | Core done | Base |
| Task allocation, partner coordination | Management panel: members, roles, tasks, achievements | AKA-35 | Seats-based |
| Lump-sum work packages | Work package module: deliverables, evidence upload, % completion | New, high value | Pro |
| GDPR consent | Consent records per participant, photo-consent flags, audit log | Extends AKA-22 | Compliance tier |
| Deadlines | Report calendar, automated reminders | New | All tiers (retention) |
| Recovery risk | Immutable activity log as evidence trail | AKA-22 | Base |

## Monetization

Highest willingness to pay sits in audit defense and lump-sum evidence. Both cut real financial loss (grant reductions, recoveries). Price on that, not on storage.

Suggested tiers:

- **Base.** One org, finance plus management, limited projects.
- **Pro.** Work-package tracking, audit export, unlimited projects.
- **Compliance.** GDPR consent vault, partner portal, advanced audit log.

Charge per seat for partner access. Consortia run 3 to 10 partners each.

## Next priorities

Two new features hit the sharpest pains. Neither exists in official tools.

1. Work-package tracker with evidence upload.
2. Report-deadline engine with reminders.
